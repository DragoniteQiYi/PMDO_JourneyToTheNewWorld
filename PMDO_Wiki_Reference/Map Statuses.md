# Map Statuses

> 来源：https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Map_Statuses

**Map Statuses** encompass all dungeon-wide battle effects and all screen-covering visual effects.

## Statuses with battle effects

### Default Weather

- Default; no battle effects

### Cloudy

- All non-Normal moves are weakened by 25%

[![](/wiki/images/2/2a/MapStatus_Cloud.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Cloud.png)

### Electric Terrain

- Electric-type moves are boosted by 1.5x
- All Pokémon on the floor cannot sleep

[![](/wiki/images/f/ff/MapStatus_ElectricTerrain.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_ElectricTerrain.png)

### Grassy Terrain

- Grass-type moves are boosted by 1.5x
- Ground-type moves are weakened by 50%
- All Pokémon on the floor recover 1/12 of their HP every 5 turns, except Flying-types

[![](/wiki/images/7/70/MapStatus_GrassyTerrain.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_GrassyTerrain.png)

### Gravity

- Drops everyone's evasion by two stages
- Prevents moves such as Telekinesis, Magnet Rise, Bounce, and Sky Drop from working
- Flying-types and Levitate immunities are removed

[![](/wiki/images/d/d5/MapStatus_Gravity.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Gravity.png)

### Hail

- All Pokémon on the floor take 1/12 of their HP as damage every 5 turns, except Ice-types

[![](/wiki/images/0/02/MapStatus_Hail.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Hail.png)

### Harsh Sun

- The weather caused by [Primal Groudon](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Primal_Groudon "Primal Groudon")'s [Desolate Land](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Desolate_Land "Desolate Land (page does not exist)") ability.
- Not implemented as of v0.7.12.

### Heavy Rain

- The weather caused by [Primal Kyogre](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Primal_Kyogre "Primal Kyogre")'s [Primordial Sea](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Primordial_Sea "Primordial Sea (page does not exist)") ability.
- Not implemented as of v0.7.12.

### Inverse

- All type matchups are reversed

[![](/wiki/images/7/7a/MapStatus_Inverse.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Inverse.png)

### Magic Room

- All held items and abilities are disabled
- [Reviver Seeds](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Reviver_Seed "Reviver Seed") cannot be activated

[![](/wiki/images/c/c4/MapStatus_MagicRoom.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_MagicRoom.png)

### Misty Terrain

- Dragon-type moves are weakened by 50%
- All Pokémon are protected from [major status conditions](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Status_Conditions%23Major_status_conditions "Status Conditions")

[![](/wiki/images/c/c2/MapStatus_MistyTerrain.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_MistyTerrain.png)

### Mysterious Distortion

[![](/wiki/images/5/5f/MapStatus_MysteriousDistortion.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_MysteriousDistortion.png)

### Psychic Terrain

- Psychic-type moves are boosted by 1.5x
- Moves will [always miss](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Forced_miss "Forced miss") at melee range

[![](/wiki/images/3/39/MapStatus_PsychicTerrain.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_PsychicTerrain.png)

### Rain

- Water-type moves are boosted by 1.5x
- Fire-type moves are weakened by 50%

[![](/wiki/images/5/51/MapStatus_Rain.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Rain.png)

### Sandstorm

- All Pokémon on the floor take 1/12 of their HP as damage every 5 turns, except Rock, Ground, and Steel-types
- The Sp. Def of Rock-types is boosted by 1/3

[![](/wiki/images/2/2a/MapStatus_Sandstorm.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Sandstorm.png)

### Something's Stirring

- The exploration team must leave the floor before it arrives, or they will be forced out of the dungeon
- Arrives in 300 turns, after the first warning

[![](/wiki/images/1/16/MapStatus_SomethingStirring.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_SomethingStirring.png)

### Sunny

- Fire-type moves are boosted by 1.5x
- Water-type moves are weakened by 50%

[![](/wiki/images/3/30/MapStatus_Sunny.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Sunny.png)

### Trick Room

- Effects on all Pokemon's Movement Speed are reversed
- The Speed stat's effect on accuracy and evasion is reversed

[![](/wiki/images/a/ab/MapStatus_TrickRoom.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_TrickRoom.png)

### Wind

- Flying-types are protected from their weaknesses

[![](/wiki/images/5/54/MapStatus_Wind.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_Wind.png)

### Wonder Room

- Physical attacks become Special attacks, and vice versa

[![](/wiki/images/d/da/MapStatus_WonderRoom.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:MapStatus_WonderRoom.png)

## Internally used statuses

These statuses are special and used by the game for specific gameplay purposes. Generally, they layer on with the other statuses instead of overwriting them.

### Rescued

- Status that handles [rescues](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Rescues "Rescues") through SOS Mail

### Shopping

- Occurs when entering [shops](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Shop "Shop") in dungeons
- Handles shop greeting and checkout
- Cannot swap leaders

### Thief

- Occurs after stealing or destroying items from a shop
- Prevents the use of [Escape Orbs](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Escape_Orb "Escape Orb")
- Prevents leader switches
- Prevents rescues
- Spawns shopkeepers near the exit

### Outlaw Clear Check

- Currently used for the outlaw mission in the debug room
- Calls SINGLE\_CHAR\_SCRIPT.OutlawClearCheck at the end of every turn

### Scanner

- Reveals all items and enemies on the floor
- Dispels [floor darkness](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Floor_darkness "Floor darkness")
- Applied by using a [Scanner Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Scanner_Orb "Scanner Orb (page does not exist)").

### Move Ban

- Bans the move from all Pokémon for the rest of the dungeon.
- Applied when a Pokémon eats a [Ban Seed](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Ban_Seed "Ban Seed").

### Monster Mansion

*This section is a **stub**. You can help PMDO Wiki by expanding it.*

- Dispels [floor darkness](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Floor_darkness "Floor darkness")

### Mysterious Force

- Prevents Orbs, Machines, and special items from working
- Useful for boss battles.
- Not used in the random boss rooms found in dungeons like [Guildmaster Trail](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Guildmaster_Trail "Guildmaster Trail") or [Secret Garden](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Secret_Garden "Secret Garden").
