# Resources for Mods

> 来源：https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Resources_for_Mods

A list of free-to-use resources that can be used in mods.

## Scripts

- [SingleEvent Scripts](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/SingleEvent_Scripts "SingleEvent Scripts") (custom dungeon events)
- [BattleEvent Scripts](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/BattleEvent_Scripts "BattleEvent Scripts (page does not exist)") (custom moves/abilities/items etc.)
- [Mission Generation Library](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Mod:Nebula%27s_Mission_Board "Mod:Nebula's Mission Board") by [MistressNebula](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/User:MistressNebula "User:MistressNebula")

## Maps

- [PMDO Explorers Maps](https://git.gay/sloth/PMDO-Explorers-Maps) for PMDO imported explorer maps

### Ground Map Props

- ["New Neighborhood November" town assets](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Mp-8VpNXNdnfI-hZk7eEiuKGscZZ3lXJ) by [JaiFain](https://bsky.app/profile/jaidonkin.bsky.social/)
- [Rescue Team-style house sprites](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/owxfyzqqegvrb96l5pq2m/House-sprites.png?rlkey=pwrkfnhq1kjs1aowvtk3csswt&st=7nizxe4v&dl=0) by oulongtea

### Pokémon Sprites

- [SpriteCollab](https://sprites.pmdcollab.org/). Most of these are in PMDO already. If you use something that wasn't, you **must** credit the artist in your mod.
- [NotSpriteCollab](https://nsc.pmdcollab.org/). Non-Pokémon sprites. You **must** credit the artist in your mod if you use these.
