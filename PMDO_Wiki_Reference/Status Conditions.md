# Status Conditions

> 来源：https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Status_Conditions

**Status conditions**, or **statuses**, are abnormal effects that may affect a Pokémon in battle. Status conditions can have either positive or negative effects.

## Major Status Conditions

The major status conditions correspond to the [non-violate status conditions](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition#Non-volatile_status) of the main series. Pokémon inflicted with major status conditions will take additional damage from moves like [Hex](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Hex "Hex (page does not exist)").

Pokémon may only have one major status condition at a time. If another status condition is attempted to be inflicted on them, they will keep the status that they currently have.

### Asleep

[![](/wiki/images/5/50/Status_Asleep.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Asleep.gif)

A sleeping [Metang](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Metang "Metang").

A **sleeping** Pokémon will be unable to act until it takes damage from an attack, or after 5 turns. Non-damaging attacks will take 1 turn off the countdown.

The only moves that may inflict sleep are direct status moves such as [spore](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Spore "Spore (page does not exist)") or [hypnosis](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Hypnosis "Hypnosis (page does not exist)"), with the sole exception of [Dire Claw](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Dire_Claw "Dire Claw (page does not exist)") that may inflict sleep. It may also be obtained through direct contact with a Pokémon that has [Effect Spore](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Effect_Spore "Effect Spore").

Sleep will last for 10 turns when inflicted by the [Yawning](#Yawning) status.

Sleep will last for 15 turns when inflicted by the [Slumber Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Slumber_Orb "Slumber Orb (page does not exist)").

Sleep will last for 20 turns when inflicted by the [Sleep Seed](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Sleep_Seed "Sleep Seed").

Sleep will last an unlimited number of turns when the [Stayaway Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Stayaway_Orb "Stayaway Orb (page does not exist)") is used, and certain Pokémon can sometimes spawn in a dungeon sleeping for an unlimited number of turns.

### Burned

[![](/wiki/images/c/cd/Status_Burned.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Burned.gif)

A burned [Whismur](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Whismur "Whismur") taking damage after attacking.

A **burned** Pokémon will have its Attack stat reduced by 1/3, and will take 1/8 of its max HP as damage every time it attacks. This status condition lasts for the entire floor, or until being removed.

[![Fire](/wiki/images/f/f0/Type_Fire.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Fire.png "Fire")Fire-type Pokémon cannot be burned.

Burns can be received from contact with a Pokémon with [Flame Body](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Flame_Body "Flame Body"), as a side effect of some
[![Fire](/wiki/images/f/f0/Type_Fire.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Fire.png "Fire")Fire-type moves, or directly with [Will-O-Wisp](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Will-O-Wisp "Will-O-Wisp (page does not exist)"). It is also gained by stepping on [lava tiles](Terrain.md "Terrain").

Burns can be cured by stepping onto [water tiles](Terrain.md "Terrain").

### Frozen

[![](/wiki/images/3/3d/Status_Frozen.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Frozen.gif)

A frozen [Tepig](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Tepig "Tepig") freeing itself by using [Flame Charge](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Flame_Charge "Flame Charge (page does not exist)").

A **frozen** Pokémon will be unable to act or take damage until 10 turns have passed. The Pokémon may be thawed early by having a [![Fire](/wiki/images/f/f0/Type_Fire.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Fire.png "Fire")Fire-type move used on it, or by using a Fire-type move of its own.

[![Ice](/wiki/images/3/35/Type_Ice.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Ice.png "Ice")Ice-type Pokémon cannot be frozen.

Freezing has no moves or abilities that inflict it directly. It may only be gotten as a side effect of [![Ice](/wiki/images/3/35/Type_Ice.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Ice.png "Ice")Ice-type moves, with the exception of [Tri Attack](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Tri_Attack "Tri Attack (page does not exist)").

Freezing will last for 30 turns when inflicted by the [Ice Seed](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Ice_Seed "Ice Seed").

Freezing will last for 20 turns when inflicted by the [Freeze Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Freeze_Orb "Freeze Orb").

### Poisoned

[![](/wiki/images/3/33/Status_Poisoned.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Poisoned.gif)

A poisoned [Pikachu](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Pikachu "Pikachu") taking damage every turn.

A **poisoned** Pokémon will take damage every turn for 6 turns. The Pokémon will take less damage if it waits in place on that turn, taking damage equivalent to 1/16 of its max HP while waiting, and 1/8 of its max HP while moving or attacking.

[![Poison](/wiki/images/9/90/Type_Poison.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Poison.png "Poison")Poison- and [![Steel](/wiki/images/8/83/Type_Steel.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Steel.png "Steel")Steel-type Pokémon cannot be poisoned or badly poisoned. The ability [corrosion](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Corrosion "Corrosion") bypasses this immunity for poison and poison inflicting moves. However, it does not allow [![Poison](/wiki/images/9/90/Type_Poison.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Poison.png "Poison")Poison-type attacking moves to damage [![Steel](/wiki/images/8/83/Type_Steel.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Steel.png "Steel")Steel-type Pokémon.

Healing items and effects will only recover half as much HP, and the Pokémon will not recover HP naturally.

Poison is a side effect of multiple [![Poison](/wiki/images/9/90/Type_Poison.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Poison.png "Poison")Poison-type moves, and can be inflicted directly with some status moves. It is also gained by stepping on [swamp tiles](Terrain.md "Terrain").

#### Badly Poisoned

[![](/wiki/images/6/68/Status_BadlyPoisoned.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_BadlyPoisoned.gif)

A badly poisoned [Deerling](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Deerling "Deerling (page does not exist)") taking increasingly more damage after attacking.

A **badly poisoned** Pokémon takes increasing damage from poison for each time they use a move. As before, the damage will start at 1/16 for standing still and 1/8 for moving, but increases by an additional 1/16 anytime the Pokémon uses a move.

Badly poisoned is most commonly inflicted through the move [Toxic](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Toxic "Toxic (page does not exist)"), but a select few [![Poison](/wiki/images/9/90/Type_Poison.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Poison.png "Poison")Poison-type moves have a chance to inflict it as a side effect.

### Paralyzed

[![](/wiki/images/3/37/Status_Paralyzed.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Paralyzed.gif)

A paralyzed [Spheal](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Spheal "Spheal") being unable to move every other turn.

A **paralyzed** Pokémon will be unable to act every other turn. This is distinct from the main series, where each turn there is a random chance to be unable to move. Paralysis lasts for 6 turns.

[![Electric](/wiki/images/f/f2/Type_Electric.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Electric.png "Electric")Electric-type Pokémon cannot be paralyzed.

Paralysis is commonly gained through contact with a Pokémon that has the ability [Static](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Static "Static"), as a side effect of some [![Electric](/wiki/images/f/f2/Type_Electric.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Electric.png "Electric")Electric-type moves, or directly with a status move.

Paralysis will last for 10 turns when inflicted by the [Petrify Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Petrify_Orb "Petrify Orb").

## General Harmful Status

These status conditions are often inflicted using status moves, through orb effects, or attacks. They come from multiple different sources and are commonly encountered. They hinder the affected Pokémon in some way.

### Confused

[![](/wiki/images/8/84/Status_Confused.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Confused.gif)

A confused [Spinda](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Spinda "Spinda") and [Psyduck](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Psyduck "Psyduck") having trouble aiming due to their facing direction being nudged.

A **confused** Pokémon has its aim disrupted for 10 turns.

In [PMDO](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/PMDO "PMDO"), before a Pokémon successfully acts, confusion has a 2/3 chance to nudge the direction a Pokémon is aiming at, either to the right (1/3) or the left (1/3). This results in a remaining 1/3 chance for the Pokémon to face its intended direction per action.

Confusion effectively cuts the accuracy of single-target moves to 33.3%, due to the chance of rotation. Wide-ranged moves, such as [Vine Whip](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Vine_Whip "Vine Whip"), are still able to hit the targeted tile and as such do not suffer from this accuracy penalty.

Additionally, the moves and items of confused Pokémon will affect both allies and enemies equally, regardless of whether they normally affect either only allies or only enemies.

Confusion will last for 15 turns when inflicted by the [Totter Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Totter_Orb "Totter Orb (page does not exist)").

### Cringe

[![](/wiki/images/b/b6/Status_Cringe.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Cringe.gif)

[Nickit](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Nickit "Nickit") cringing due to [Tyrogue](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Tyrogue "Tyrogue")'s [Fake Out](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Fake_Out "Fake Out (page does not exist)").

A **cringing** (or "flinching") Pokémon is unable to use moves or items for one turn, but can still move. This status effect cannot be inflicted if the target cringed on the previous turn.

Cringe will last for 3 turns if inflicted by the [Fear Wand](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Fear_Wand "Fear Wand (page does not exist)").

### Immobilized

[![](/wiki/images/7/72/Status_Immobilized.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Immobilized.gif)

An immobilized [Raboot](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Raboot "Raboot") returning to its original position after using [Quick Attack](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Quick_Attack "Quick Attack (page does not exist)").

An **immobilized** Pokémon is unable to move for 5 turns, though it may still attack and use items freely. While immobilized, moves that would normally bring the user forward, such as [Quick Attack](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Quick_Attack "Quick Attack (page does not exist)"), will instead return the Pokémon to its original location.

[![Ghost](/wiki/images/e/ee/Type_Ghost.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Ghost.png "Ghost")Ghost-type Pokémon cannot be immobilized.

Immobilization will last for 20 turns when inflicted by the [Foe-Hold Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Foe-Hold_Orb "Foe-Hold Orb").

## Specialized Harmful Status

*This section is a **stub**. You can help PMDO Wiki by expanding it.*

These harmful statuses are typically inflicted only by specific moves and/or abilities.

### In Love

[![](/wiki/images/8/8c/Status_InLove.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_InLove.gif)

[Poochyena](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Poochyena "Poochyena") in love with [Skitty](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Skitty "Skitty") due to [Cute Charm](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Cute_Charm "Cute Charm").

A Pokémon who is **in love** (or "infatuated") cannot use moves or items on the Pokémon who inflicted the status for 10 turns. If it does, the attack will [automatically miss](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Forced_miss "Forced miss"). This status can normally only be inflicted on Pokémon of the opposite gender, unless the [Gender Unlock](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Gender_Unlock "Gender Unlock") mod is enabled. This effect is inflicted by the move [Attract](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Attract "Attract (page does not exist)") and the ability [Cute Charm](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Cute_Charm "Cute Charm").

### Disabled

Upon being **Disabled**, the last move used by that Pokémon becomes unable to be selected for the rest of the floor.
This effect is inflicted by the moves [Disable](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Disable "Disable (page does not exist)") and [Imprison](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Imprison "Imprison (page does not exist)"), as well as the [Seal Trap](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Seal_Trap "Seal Trap (page does not exist)").

### Taunted

[![](/wiki/images/d/db/Status_Taunted.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Taunted.png)

A taunted [Bonsly](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Bonsly "Bonsly") unable to select any status moves.

A **taunted** Pokémon is unable to select its status moves, and must use attacking moves instead. However, the Pokémon is not forced to attack, and may still pass its turn or use an item instead. This effect is inflicted by the move [Taunt](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Taunt "Taunt (page does not exist)") and lasts for 15 turns.

### Tormented

A **tormented** Pokémon cannot use the same move twice in a row. This effect is inflicted by the move [Torment](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Torment "Torment (page does not exist)") and lasts for 25 turns.

### Fire Spin/Whirlpool/Sand Tomb

Upon being hit by the appropriate move, the Pokémon becomes trapped and is unable to move from its current location, but may still use moves and items. At the end of each of the Pokemon's turns, it will also take damage equal to 1/8 of its max HP.

These effects are inflicted by the moves [Fire Spin](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Fire_Spin "Fire Spin"), [Whirlpool](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Whirlpool "Whirlpool (page does not exist)"), and [Sand Tomb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Sand_Tomb "Sand Tomb (page does not exist)"), and last for 3 turns.

These statuses function identically but are considered separate, and multiple may be active at once.

### Leech Seed

[![](/wiki/images/5/5b/Status_Leech_Seed.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Leech_Seed.png)

A [Bulbasaur](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Bulbasaur "Bulbasaur") using leech seed on [Marshtomp](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Marshtomp "Marshtomp").

A Pokémon inflicted with **Leech Seed** will lose 1/12 of its max HP at the end of every turn, while the nearest enemy Pokémon will recover the same amount. Leech Seed wears off once the affected Pokémon is at least 5 tiles away from any enemy Pokémon, but will otherwise last indefinitely.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Leech Seed](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Leech_Seed "Leech Seed").

[![Grass](/wiki/images/4/4c/Type_Grass.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Type_Grass.png "Grass")Grass-type Pokémon cannot be seeded.

### Embargo

**Embargo** prevents the affected Pokémon from using or throwing any items for 50 turns. Held items may still be equipped, but their effects will not activate.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Embargo](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Embargo "Embargo (page does not exist)").

### Exposed

[![](/wiki/images/0/07/Status_Exposed.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Exposed.png)

An exposed [Magneton](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Magneton "Magneton") being damaged by [Sludge Wave](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Sludge_Wave "Sludge Wave (page does not exist)").

An **Exposed** Pokémon loses all of its resistances and immunities, taking neutral damage from them instead. Additionally, any increases to the Pokémon's evasion are ignored. This effect lasts for 30 turns, and is inflicted by the move [Foresight](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Foresight "Foresight (page does not exist)").

### Heal Block

**Heal Block** prevents the the affected Pokémon from recovering HP by any means for 50 turns. Healing items or moves may still be used, but no HP will be recovered.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Heal Block](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Heal_Block "Heal Block (page does not exist)").

### Paused

While **Paused**, the Pokemon is rendered completely incapable of taking any action; being unable to move, attack, or use any items. Paused primarily exists as a self-inflicted status.

If inflicted by the ability [Truant](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Truant "Truant") or the [Iron Ball](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Iron_Ball "Iron Ball (page does not exist)") held item, this status will skip 1 of the user's turns.

If inflicted by a move with a recharging period, such as [Hyper Beam](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Hyper_Beam "Hyper Beam (page does not exist)") or [Giga Impact](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Giga_Impact "Giga Impact (page does not exist)"), this status will skip 3 of the user's turns.

If a Pokemon with Truant or an Iron Ball uses a move with a recharge time, Truant/Iron Ball's status will override the move's status, only skipping 1 turn instead.

### Perish Count

The first time the Pokémon is hit by the move [Perish Song](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Perish_Song "Perish Song (page does not exist)"), they are inflicted with the **Perish Count** status. This status starts at 3, and lowers by 1 with each successive use of Perish Song, causing the Pokémon to faint once it reaches 0. The Perish Count has no timer, and will last the entire floor unless cured.

### Yawning

Upon being hit by the move [Yawn](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Yawn "Yawn (page does not exist)"), the affected Pokemon is given the **Yawning** status. After 3 turns pass, the Pokémon will fall [asleep](#Asleep) for 10 turns.

This status cannot be applied if the target already has a major status condition, and will also be removed if one is inflicted before 3 turns pass.

## Beneficial Status

*This section is a **stub**. You can help PMDO Wiki by expanding it.*

Status effects that benefit the Pokémon that have them.

### Reflect

**Reflect** halves all physical damage the Pokémon takes for 10 turns.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Reflect](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Reflect "Reflect (page does not exist)").

### Light Screen

**Light Screen** halves all special damage the Pokémon takes for 10 turns.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Light Screen](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Light_Screen "Light Screen (page does not exist)").

### Aqua Ring

**Aqua Ring** recovers 1/8 of the Pokémon's max HP at the end of every turn, for 10 turns.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Aqua Ring](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Aqua_Ring "Aqua Ring").

### Wish

**Wish** counts down for 3 turns, then recovers 1/2 of the Pokémon's max HP.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Wish](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Wish "Wish (page does not exist)").

### Safeguard

**Safeguard** prevents the Pokémon from being inflicted with negative status effects for 15 turns.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Safeguard](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Safeguard "Safeguard (page does not exist)").

### Sure Shot

**Sure Shot** guarantees that all moves used by the Pokémon for the next 25 turns cannot miss.
This effect is inflicted by the moves [Lock-On](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Lock-On "Lock-On (page does not exist)") and [Mind Reader](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Mind_Reader "Mind Reader (page does not exist)"), as well as the [All-Aim Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/All-Aim_Orb "All-Aim Orb").

### Miracle Eye

**Miracle Eye** allows the Pokémon to ignore the type resistances and immunities of enemy Pokémon, as well as any positive changes to their evasion, for 10 turns.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Miracle Eye](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Miracle_Eye "Miracle Eye").

### Invisible

[![](/wiki/images/d/db/Status_Invisible.gif)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Invisible.gif)

An invisible [Shedinja](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Shedinja "Shedinja (page does not exist)") being ignored by enemies.

The **Invisible** status causes the Pokémon to disappear, becoming undetectable by enemy Pokémon for the next 30 turns. An invisible Pokémon in the Player's party will become transparent, while an invisible enemy will disappear completely.

Invisible Pokémon may still attack and use items without losing their invisibility.

While invisible Pokémon will not be targeted by the AI, they may still be damaged if they are within range of an attack launched at another Pokémon. Likewise, while the player can not see an invisible enemy, they may still damage them by guessing their location and attacking in that direction.

This effect is inflicted by the [Vanish Wand](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Vanish_Wand "Vanish Wand (page does not exist)").

## On-hit Status

*This section is a **stub**. You can help PMDO Wiki by expanding it.*

Status effects that have some effect when the Pokémon is hit by an opponent. The effects of these statuses will not trigger if the user faints from the attack.

### Grudge

[![](/wiki/images/f/f0/Status_grudge.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_grudge.png)

A [Mismagius](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Mismagius "Mismagius") holding a grudge.

While the Pokémon is holding a **grudge**, any enemy Pokémon that damages it with a move will lose 3pp each from all of its moves.

Attacking the Pokémon with a regular attack or status move will not trigger Grudge's PP loss.

This effect lasts for 25 turns, and is inflicted by the move [Grudge](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Grudge "Grudge (page does not exist)") and the [Grudge Trap](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Effect_Tile%23Traps "Effect Tile").

### Destiny Bond

After successfully hitting an enemy Pokemon with the move [Destiny Bond](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Destiny_Bond "Destiny Bond (page does not exist)"), the user of that move is inflicted with the **Destiny Bond** status for 3 turns. Anytime the user is damaged by an attack during this time, the targeted Pokémon will take the same amount of damage.

Destiny Bond's shared damage will only apply to attacks and items; damage the user takes from traps or status effects will not damage the target.

### Mini-Counter

[![](/wiki/images/b/b3/Status_Mini_Counter.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Status_Mini_Counter.png)

A [Chansey](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Chansey "Chansey") with Mini-Counter reflecting damage back to an attacking [Zangoose](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Zangoose "Zangoose").

**Mini-Counter** causes all attacks that hit the inflicted Pokémon to deal the same amount of damage to the attacker. Mini-counter's damage will only apply to attacks and items, not indirect damage from status effects.

This effect lasts for 50 turns, and is inflicted by the [Rebound Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Rebound_Orb "Rebound Orb").

### Counter

While **Counter** is active, the Pokémon will retaliate against any Pokémon that deals physical damage to it, dealing double the damage back to the attacker.

This effect lasts for 4 turns, and is gained by using the move [Counter](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Counter "Counter (page does not exist)").

### Mirror Coat

While **Mirror Coat** is active, the Pokémon will retaliate against any Pokémon that deals special damage to it, dealing double the damage back to the attacker.

This effect lasts for 4 turns, and is gained by using the move [Mirror Coat](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Mirror_Coat "Mirror Coat (page does not exist)").

Unlike in the main series, both **Counter** and **Mirror Coat** may be active at the same time due to lasting several turns.

### Magic Coat

While **Magic Coat** is active, any status move used on the Pokémon will be reflected back to the attacker. Both harmful and beneficial statuses will be reflected. This effect is gained by using the move [Magic Coat](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Magic_Coat "Magic Coat (page does not exist)") or by using the [Mirror Orb](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Mirror_Orb "Mirror Orb").

If inflicted by Magic Coat, it will last for 15 turns.

If inflicted by the Mirror Orb, it will last for 30 turns.

## Neutral Status

*This section is a **stub**. You can help PMDO Wiki by expanding it.*

Status effects that may have negative and positive side effects, or have effects that may situationally be helpful or situationally harmful.

### Sleepless

**Sleepless** prevents the Pokémon from being inflicted with the [Asleep](#Asleep) or [Yawning](#Yawning) statuses for the next 50 turns. While usually beneficial, it also prevents the Pokemon from using the move [Rest](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Rest "Rest") to recover HP. Sleepless is inflicted by the move [Uproar](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Uproar "Uproar (page does not exist)").

### Ingrain

**Ingrain** recovers 1/6 of the Pokémon's max HP at the end of every turn, for 10 turns. The Pokémon is unable to move while ingrained, but may still use attacks and items.
This effect is inflicted by the move [Ingrain](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Ingrain "Ingrain (page does not exist)").

### Chasing

After successfully hitting an enemy Pokémon with the move [Pursuit](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Pursuit "Pursuit (page does not exist)") or [Block](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Block "Block (page does not exist)"), the user of that move is inflicted with the **Chasing** status for 5 turns.

At the start of the user's turns, if they are 2 or more tiles away from the target Pokémon, they will randomly teleport to a tile adjacent to the target. While this does prevent the enemy Pokémon from escaping, it can also cause the user to teleport into unfavorable positions if other enemies are nearby.

### Bind

After being hit by the move [Bind](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Bind "Bind (page does not exist)") or [Wrap](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Wrap "Wrap (page does not exist)"), the Pokémon is inflicted with the **Bind** status and is unable to move, attack, or use items for 3 turns, taking damage from the enemy Pokémon at the end of each turn. The enemy Pokémon, however, is also unable to move or cancel the attack during this time, leaving them open to being attacked by the target's allies.

### Decoy

After being hit by the move [Substitute](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Substitute "Substitute (page does not exist)") or consuming a [![Seed_Yellow.png](/wiki/images/6/69/Seed_Yellow.png)](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/File:Seed_Yellow.png "Seed_Yellow.png") [Decoy Seed](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Decoy_Seed "Decoy Seed"), the Pokémon is inflicted with the **Decoy** status. Their overworld sprite changes to a decoy doll. The Pokémon is still able to attack and use moves, but every single attack made will target it. The AI of enemies will also prioritize the decoy.

This status may be used to draw attention to protect more vulnerable teammates from attacks. However, it can be forced onto party members, which if they are not particularly durable, can be very dangerous.

### Follow Me

This effect is self-inflicted after using the move [Follow Me](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Follow_Me "Follow Me (page does not exist)"). All moves targeting the Pokémon's allies will be redirected to hit the Follow Me user instead. This may be beneficial for shielding team members, but may also result in the user being fainted from taking too much damage.

### Roosting

After using [roost](https://wiki.pmdo.pmdcollab.org/wiki/Roost "Roost (page does not exist)"), the user becomes **roosting**. If the Pokémon is a flying type, they will temporarily lose the flying typing. The sprite also changes to the Pokémon's sleeping sprite. This effect wears off when the Pokémon moves.

This can be beneficial to removing a flying type weakness, but may also be harmful by eliminating flying type resistances and the ground immunity.
